Recognize a document

Pagio recognizes the type of a document (invoice, contract, payslip, statement, tax notice) to help you file it.

PremiumOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose a PDF or a photo of a document.
  2. Start the analysis.
  3. Copy, save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

What is it for?

Filing your papers quickly and finding the right document.

Is the document kept?

No, it is analysed then deleted from the server.

Is Recognize a document free?

Included in Premium, with AI credits every month.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

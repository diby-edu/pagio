Page numbers

Add page numbers to a thesis, report or file, at the top or bottom, as "1" or "Page 1 of 10".

FreeOn AndroidOnline

Use it nowDownload the Android app

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start from another number?

Yes, choose the first number and the first numbered page.

Where are numbers placed?

Top or bottom, left, centre or right.

Is Page numbers free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

How to do it

  1. Open the tool in the app.
  2. Follow the on-screen instructions.
  3. Save or share the document.

Frequently asked questions

Is the scan saved as PDF?

As images (JPG) by default; saving as PDF is part of Premium.

Can I scan several pages?

Yes, pages are stored in a folder: page 1, page 2…

Is Scan free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

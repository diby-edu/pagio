PDF to PowerPoint

Turn a PDF into a PowerPoint presentation, one slide per page, to project or complete it.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is the text editable?

Yes with the editable option; otherwise each page becomes a slide faithful to the original.

Does it work with Google Slides?

Yes, the .pptx file also opens in Google Slides.

Is PDF to PowerPoint free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

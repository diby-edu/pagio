OCR: searchable PDF

Turn a scanned PDF (an image) into a document whose text can be selected, copied and searched.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are recognized?

English, French and the main Latin-alphabet languages.

Does the document look different?

No, the recognized text is added invisibly under the image.

Is OCR: searchable PDF free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

Verify signatures

Check that a digitally signed PDF has not been changed since it was signed, and who signed it.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

What does the check show?

The signer, the date and whether the document was changed afterwards.

Do I need an account?

No, the check happens on your phone.

Is Verify signatures free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

Organize pages

See every page as a thumbnail, then drag pages into place, rotate, duplicate or remove the ones you do not need.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see the pages before saving?

Yes, every page is shown as a thumbnail while you move it.

Does the quality change?

No, pages are moved as they are, with no loss.

Is Organize pages free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

Merge PDF

Put invoices, receipts or chapters together in a single PDF file and choose the order of the documents.

FreeOn AndroidOnline

Use it nowDownload the Android app

How to do it

  1. Choose one or more PDFs.
  2. Set the order and options.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge?

As many as you like on the phone; online, the limit depends on your plan.

Are my files uploaded?

No: merging happens in your browser or on your phone.

Is Merge PDF free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

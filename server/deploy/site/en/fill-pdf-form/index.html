Fill a form

Fill in a PDF form (application, registration, declaration) by tapping the fields, then save it.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

What if the PDF has no fields?

Use Edit PDF to write anywhere.

Can I sign it afterwards?

Yes, with the Sign PDF tool.

Is Fill a form free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

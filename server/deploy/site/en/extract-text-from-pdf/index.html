How to do it

  1. Choose a PDF or a photo of a document.

Frequently asked questions

What if my PDF is a scan?

Pagio reads the text in the image (OCR) before extracting it.

What format is the result?

A text file (.txt) you can also copy directly.

Is Extract text free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

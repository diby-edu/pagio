PDF to Excel

Get the tables of a statement, report or invoice into an Excel workbook, ready for calculations.

FreeOn Android

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

One sheet per page?

Yes if you want (one sheet per page option).

Can numbers still be calculated?

Yes, amounts are recognized as numbers.

Is PDF to Excel free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

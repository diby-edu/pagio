Frequently asked questions

Do images keep their quality?

Yes, they are extracted exactly as stored in the PDF.

Difference with PDF to JPG?

Here you get the images alone, not the whole page.

Is Extract images free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

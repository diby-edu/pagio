ID card front and back

Put the front and back of your ID card (national ID, passport, driving licence, residence permit) on a single A4 page, real size, like a photocopy.

FreeOnline

Use it now

How to do it

  1. Take a photo of the front, then the back of the card.
  2. Choose real or enlarged size, colour or black and white.
  3. Download the A4 page and print it.

Frequently asked questions

What size will the card be?

The real size of a card (85.6 × 54 mm), like a photocopy. You can also choose an enlarged version that is easier to read.

Are my photos sent over the internet?

No: the page is made in your browser, your photos never leave your device.

Can I put two copies on the page?

Yes, at real size you can place two copies side by side to save paper.

Is ID card front and back free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

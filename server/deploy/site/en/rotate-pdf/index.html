Rotate

Turn an upside-down scan or landscape pages the right way, by 90° or 180°, then save the fixed PDF.

FreeOn AndroidOnline

Use it nowDownload the Android app

How to do it

  1. Choose one or more PDFs.
  2. Set the order and options.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is the change permanent?

Yes, the saved PDF keeps the new orientation everywhere.

Can I rotate a single page?

Yes, choose which pages to rotate.

Is Rotate free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

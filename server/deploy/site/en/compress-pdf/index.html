Compress PDF

Shrink a heavy PDF to send it by e-mail, WhatsApp or to an application site that limits file size.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose one or more PDFs.
  2. Set the order and options.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can I aim for a specific size (1 MB, 2 MB)?

Yes, choose the compression level; Pagio shows the resulting size.

Does quality drop?

Text stays sharp; only images are lightened according to the chosen level.

Is Compress PDF free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.

Redact

Hide an account number, an address or a name for good before sharing a document: the hidden text is removed.

FreeOn Android

Download the Android app

Available in the Android app; the online version is coming soon.

How to do it

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Adjust the options if needed.
  3. Save or share the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can the hidden text be recovered?

No, it is truly removed from the file, not just covered.

What can be hidden?

The words you list, and in one go every e-mail address, phone number or IBAN.

Is Redact free?

Free, with a daily limit; unlimited with Premium.

Are my documents safe?

Yes: most processing happens on your phone or in your browser. When a document goes through the Pagio server, it is deleted right after.
